Across the street
It fights by itself. When a spirit's gauge fills, its ability lights up: tap it to use it now, or leave Auto on and it fires as soon as it can. Tap an enemy to focus everyone on it.
The math

Stats at level L = 10 + spread × (L − 1). HP Grit × 8 + 20. Hit the better of Brawn or Wits − a third of the target's Grit, min 1. Acts every 4 s × 10 ÷ Pace. Crit Knack ÷ 200, ×2. Element yours quells theirs ×1.5, theirs quells yours ×0.75. Line the ally above feeds you ×1.25. Gauge +25 per own action, +10 per hit taken; full at 100. Abilities (passive · tap): Light: strikes first · Flare, Wits × 0.8 to all, dazzled 5 s (half damage). Toll: rings every 10 s for Wits × 0.3 · Great Toll, Wits × 1.2 to all, stunned 2 s. Sweep: 30% splash · Clean Sweep, Brawn to all. Steam: burn 5 s on hit · Scald, ×2 to the focus target, burn 8 s. Carry: +25% HP · Bulwark, shields the line for Grit × 2 each, 6 s. Comfort: heals every 5 s · Lullaby, heals all Heart × 1.5, enemies lose 2 s. Tame a beaten rogue if your best Heart ≥ its level + 10; in a group, only the last one standing can be tamed.

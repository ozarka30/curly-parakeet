The math, so you can argue with it

Stats at level L = 10 + spread × (L − 1). HP = Grit × 8 + 20. Hit = the better of Brawn (strikes) and Wits (spirit) − a third of the target's Grit, never under 1. Acts every 4 s × 10 ÷ Pace (Pace 20 acts every 2 s). Crit = Knack ÷ 200, double damage. Element: your affinity quells theirs ×1.5, theirs quells yours ×0.75 (Bloom → Clay → Tide → Ember → Frost → Bloom). Line: the ally above you feeds your affinity → your hits ×1.25. Heart heals the weakest ally Heart × 0.4 every 5 s. Abilities: Light strikes first and +10% crit · Toll rings every 10 s, Wits × 0.5 to every enemy · Sweep splashes 30% to a second enemy · Steam burns Wits × 0.2 a second for 5 s · Carry +25% HP · Comfort heals the party Heart × 0.3 every 5 s and tames easier. Taming: a beaten rogue is tamed if your best Heart ≥ its level + 10 (Comfort: + 0), else it breaks.
